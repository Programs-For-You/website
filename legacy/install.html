How to install

Get our apps onto your computer in a few minutes. Pick your computer:

On a Mac

All three apps work on a Mac. You only do the security step once per app.

Why does my Mac say it can’t check the app? Apple only “verifies” apps from developers who pay for its $99/year developer program. Ours are free and we haven’t paid for that yet, so macOS warns you and asks you to approve the app once. That warning shows up for any unverified app, not because something is wrong with ours. Only approve apps you downloaded from programsforyou.com.

  1. 1

    Type your email and download

    Go to Downloads, type your email (no password needed), and click Download next to the app you want.

  2. 2

    Open the download

    Open your Downloads folder (in Finder, or click the download arrow in your browser). Double-click the .zip file and it turns into the app.

  3. 3

    Move it to Applications

    Drag the app into the Applications folder in Finder’s sidebar. Keeping it there means it works properly and updates cleanly.

  4. 4

    Open it, then click Done

    Double-click the app. Your Mac shows a message saying it couldn’t check the app. Click Done. Don’t click Move to Trash.

    “School Schedule” Not Opened

    DoneMove to Trash

    Click Done

  5. 5

    Allow it in System Settings

    Open the Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security. Next to the message about the app, click Open Anyway. Type your Mac password, then click Open Anyway one more time.

    Privacy & Security
    Security
    “School Schedule” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway

    Click Open Anyway

    Tip: the Open Anyway button only shows for about an hour after you try to open the app. If it’s gone, double-click the app again, then come back here.

    On macOS 14 Sonoma there’s a shortcut: in Applications, right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose Open, then click Open again.

  6. 6

    Find the button at the top of your screen

    Look in the top-right of your screen for the app’s new button. Click it to get started. Each app page has instructions: School Schedule · Email Assistant · Screen Assistant.

Mac troubleshooting

I can’t see the button at the top of my screen.

When the menu bar is full, macOS hides some icons, especially on MacBooks with a notch. Quit a few other menu-bar apps, or hold ⌘ and drag icons to make room. On macOS 26, also check System Settings → Menu Bar and make sure the app is allowed.

It says the app “is damaged and can’t be opened.”

Drag the app to the Trash, download it again, and open the .zip with a normal double-click (not a different unzip app). If it still happens, contact us.

There’s no Open Anyway button.

Double-click the app once more and click Done, then go straight back to System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll all the way down. If it still isn’t there, a school or work Mac may be managed by an IT team that blocks outside apps. Ask them, or use a personal Mac.

Comfortable with Terminal? This does the same thing as Open Anyway (change the name for the other app):

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/School Schedule.app"

My Mac asks for my password to use the “keychain.”

That’s Email Assistant opening the app password it saved in your Mac’s Keychain. Type your Mac password and click Always Allow. You may see this once after each update.

Screen Assistant says it can’t see my screen.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and turn on Screen Assistant, then quit it and open it again. If it’s already on but still doesn’t work, select Screen Assistant in that list, click − to remove it, then open Screen Assistant and allow it again.

How do I update an app?

You don’t have to do anything. Our apps update themselves: they check for new versions now and then, download them quietly, and install them the next time you step away from your computer for a couple of minutes (or when you quit the app). It only takes a second, and all your settings stay. You can turn this off in the app’s Settings under Install updates automatically, and then it asks you first.

Automatic updates work in School Schedule 1.3.2, Email Assistant 1.2.1, Screen Assistant 1.1.1, and newer. If you have an older version, download the app once more from Downloads, drag it into Applications, and choose Replace. After that, you’ll never need to download it again. Keep the app in your Applications folder so it can update itself.

How do I uninstall an app?

Click the app’s button at the top of your screen and choose Quit. Then drag the app from Applications to the Trash.

Does it work on Windows, iPhone, iPad, or Chromebook?

School Schedule works on Windows: see the Windows guide. Windows versions of Email Assistant and Screen Assistant are coming soon. iPhone, iPad, and Chromebook aren’t supported right now.

On a Windows PC

School Schedule works on Windows 10 and 11. Its Windows version is Owen Wang’s School Schedule Manager: a small bar in the corner of your screen with your class, the time left, and what’s next. Windows versions of Email Assistant and Screen Assistant are coming soon.

Why does Windows warn me about it? Windows warns you about any app whose maker hasn’t paid for a code-signing certificate, which costs hundreds of dollars a year. School Schedule Manager is safe, and its code is public on GitHub for anyone to check. You only see the warnings while installing.

Windows troubleshooting

There’s no Run anyway button.

Click More info first. Run anyway only shows up after that, next to Don’t run.

Still no button? The computer is set to only allow apps from the Microsoft Store. On your own computer, open Settings, search for Choose where to get apps, and set it to Anywhere. On a school or work computer, only the people who manage it can change this.

Windows says “Smart App Control blocked an app.”

Smart App Control is a setting on some Windows 11 computers that blocks every app that isn’t signed, with no Run anyway button. It’s in Windows Security → App & browser control. On many computers, once it’s turned off it can’t be turned back on without resetting Windows, so ask a parent before changing it, or contact us.

My browser or antivirus deleted the file.

Some browsers and antivirus apps are extra careful with new apps that aren’t signed. Download it again and choose Keep (see step 1). If your antivirus keeps removing it, you can tell it to allow the file, or call us and we’ll help.

I can’t find the bar.

Look in the bottom-left corner of your screen, just above the taskbar. If it’s hidden, find the app’s icon by the clock (click the ^ arrow), right-click it, and choose Show Window. If the app isn’t running, open it from the Start menu.

It says it’s a short day when it isn’t.

Do step 7 to fix the calendar. You only need to do it once.

How do I make it open when my computer starts?

Run the Setup file again and tick Create a shortcut to launch the app on computer startup. Running Setup again resets the app’s settings, so you may need to add your classes again and redo step 7.

How do I update it?

The Windows version doesn’t update itself yet. When there’s a new version, it’ll be on our Downloads page: download the new Setup file and run it, just like the first time. Installing it resets the app’s settings, so you may need to add your classes again.

How do I uninstall it?

Right-click the app’s icon by the clock and choose Quit Application. Then open Settings → Apps → Installed apps (on Windows 10, Settings → Apps → Apps & features), find WHS School Schedule Manager, and choose Uninstall.

Is the Windows version the same as the Mac version?

Not quite. The Windows version is Owen Wang’s original School Schedule Manager, and the Mac version is built from it with extras. Both show your classes, the time left, and your day’s schedule. Only the Mac version has the homework list and tonight’s plan, the quick homework box with AI, the “Got homework?” reminder after class, the Short day and Advisory buttons, and automatic updates. New Windows versions show up on our Downloads page and Owen’s GitHub page.

What about Email Assistant and Screen Assistant?

They’re Mac only for now. Windows versions are coming soon. Join our email list on the Downloads page and we’ll tell you when they’re ready.
