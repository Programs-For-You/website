Open with your Netlify key

This page reads your forms straight from Netlify, so it needs a Netlify access token (a long password made just for this). It stays in this browser and is only sent to Netlify.

How to get your token (one time)

  1. Go to app.netlify.com → User settings → Applications.
  2. Under Personal access tokens, click New access token.
  3. Name it Programs For You admin, pick how long it lasts, and click Generate token.
  4. Copy it and paste it on the left. Don’t share it with anyone: it can change your Netlify account.
