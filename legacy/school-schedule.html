Features

Everything for your school day

Live class timer

A timer counts down exactly what’s left in class, like 11:42. It’s in the menu bar too, with seconds if you want them.

WHS built in, any school works

Comes with the WHS bell schedule, the Day 1–4 rotation, and advisory. Different school? Change the times and classes in a few steps.

Just type your homework

After class, type something like “bio worksheet due Fri, math test Thursday.” It figures out the class, the due date, and how long it’ll take.

A homework plan for you

The Plan tab spreads your homework over the nights before it’s due, and gives tests study time on the nights right before.

Homework from Schoology

Link your Schoology calendar and your assignments show up by themselves. Check them off as you finish. How to link it

Overlay bar or floating card

Keep it on top of your windows. The overlay bar sits in a corner, see-through, and opens with your timeline and homework when you hover on it.

Asks after every class

When a class ends, a notification asks if you got homework. Type it right into the notification. Reminders come the night before, too.

After-school events

Add practices and clubs. It counts down your free time until they start.

Make it yours

Six themes or your own colors, an icon for every class, 12- or 24-hour time, sharp or round corners, and a code-style font.

Stays on your Mac

Your classes and homework are saved on your Mac only. No sign-in inside the app.

Instructions

How to use School Schedule

School Schedule is coming soon. These steps show how it will work when it launches.

  1. 1

    Install it

    Download the app and open it the first time by following the install guide. It only takes a few clicks.

  2. 2

    Pick your school

    The first time it opens, choose WHS to use the built-in schedule, or My school is different to type in your own times.

  3. 3

    Different school? Enter your times

    Type the start and end time for each period. If your school rotates days (like Day 1–4 or A/B days), pick how many days are in the rotation and which classes meet on each day.

  4. 4

    Add your classes

    Type the name of each class, like Biology or Algebra II. You can change them anytime in Settings.

  5. 5

    Click the clock at the top of your screen

    The button shows your current class and a timer with what’s left. Click it to see today’s schedule, your homework plan, and your homework list. Right-click it for more options.

  6. 6

    Type your homework after class

    When a class ends, a notification asks “Got homework?” Click Add Homework and type it, like worksheet due Friday. Or click the clock and type in the Got homework? Type it here box at the bottom. You can put several things in one line: bio worksheet due fri, math test thursday, read ch 5. Press Return.

    If you don’t say the class, it uses the class you’re in or just left. Say “test” or “quiz” and it plans study time. Made a mistake? Click Undo.

  7. 7

    Follow your plan

    Open the Plan tab. Tonight shows what to do, how long each thing should take, and when to start (after school and practice). Check things off as you finish. Big projects and test studying are split over a few nights.

    Something will take longer? Right-click it in the Homework tab and choose How long will it take? To change how much homework time you get each night, go to Settings → Reminders → Homework plan.

  8. 8

    Link Schoology (optional)

    In Schoology on the web, click Calendar, then Export, and copy the link (it starts with webcal://). In School Schedule, open Settings → Schoology, paste it, and click Link Schoology. Your Schoology homework shows up in the Homework and Plan tabs. Click the circle next to one when you’re done. Step-by-step instructions

    It syncs every 30 minutes, or click Sync now in the Homework tab. Right-click an assignment to change its class or open it in Schoology. Needs version 1.3.7 or newer.

  9. 9

    Keep it on your screen

    Click the pin at the top of the panel (or right-click the clock → Keep on Screen). A small window stays on top of your other windows with your class timer and tonight’s homework. Drag it by the top to move it. Click the arrows to make it smaller, or the pin again to put it away.

  10. 10

    Try the overlay bar

    Right-click the clock → On Screen → Overlay Bar. A bar shows up in the bottom-left corner with your class, a progress bar, the percent done, the time left, and what’s next. Clicks go right through it. Hover over it for a second and it opens with today’s timeline, your plan, your homework, and the homework box. Hide (10s) gets it out of the way for ten seconds. Change its corner, see-through amount, and size in Settings → Look → On screen.

  11. 11

    Make it yours

    In Settings → Look, pick a theme or Custom colors, 24-hour time, sharp corners, and a code-style font. In Settings → Classes, click a class’s circle to pick its icon. Right-click the clock → Show Seconds for a timer like 11:42 in the menu bar. You can also add after-school events (on weekdays or one date), change reminders, and pick your advisory day.

    New school year? Paste the yearly info code (the same one the Windows app uses) in Settings → School, or click Copy this year’s code to share yours.

Tips and fixes

I can’t see the button at the top of my screen.

When the menu bar is full, macOS hides some icons, especially on MacBooks with a notch. Quit a few other menu-bar apps, or hold ⌘ and drag icons to make room. On macOS 26, also check System Settings → Menu Bar and make sure School Schedule is allowed.

I don’t get the “Got homework?” notification.

Open System Settings → Notifications → School Schedule and turn on Allow Notifications. Choose Alerts so it stays until you answer. Also check that Settings → Reminders → After class is on in the app.

Today’s schedule is wrong (it says short day or advisory when it isn’t).

Use the Short day and Advisory buttons right under the date in School Schedule’s window to turn either one on or off for today. You can also right-click the button at the top of your screen and choose Short Day Today or Advisory Day Today. Click Reset to go back to the school calendar. It only changes that one day.

On Windows, it says November 19, 20, 23 or 24 is a short day.

Those early-release days are only for grades K-8. The Mac version is already fixed. To fix the Windows version:

In School Schedule Manager, click Settings, then Edit Yearly Info. Paste this code where it says “Paste import code here,” then click Save and Close:

Year:2026;Adv:3;Limit:C286;NoS:CFD9EBECF0FDGFGGGHI5I6I9IAIBIC010I262728292A4855565758597F;Short:CHFCHAI40D0J103C50848586

Can I get it for Windows?

Yes, for School Schedule! Its Windows version is Owen Wang’s original School Schedule Manager. Get it on our Downloads page: check the box to agree to Owen Wang’s Terms of Service, click Windows Download, open the Setup file, and follow the steps. Its source code is on GitHub: github.com/un-undefined/school-schedule-manager. Windows versions of Email Assistant and Screen Assistant are coming soon.

Is the Windows version the same as the Mac version?

Not quite. The Windows version is Owen Wang’s original School Schedule Manager, and the Mac version is built from it with extras. Both show your classes, the time left, and your day’s schedule. Only the Mac version has the homework list and tonight’s plan, the quick homework box with AI, the “Got homework?” reminder after class, the Short day and Advisory buttons, and automatic updates. New Windows versions show up on our Downloads page and Owen’s GitHub page.

Does it use AI? Is my homework sent anywhere?

On Macs with Apple Intelligence turned on, it uses the AI built into your Mac to estimate how long things take. Everything stays on your Mac. On other Macs, it reads your note with its own built-in rules.

The day number is wrong today.

Snow days and special schedules can throw off the rotation. Open Settings and set today’s day number. It will count from there.

How do I quit it?

Click its button at the top of your screen and choose Quit.

Where is my information saved?

On your Mac only. Nothing about your classes or homework is sent to us. If you link Schoology, the app downloads your calendar straight from Schoology.

My Schoology homework isn’t showing up.

Open Settings → Schoology and click Sync now. If it says the link didn’t work, copy it again from Schoology (Calendar → Export) and paste the whole link. Only assignments due today or later come in. If you can’t find Export, your school may have turned it off.
