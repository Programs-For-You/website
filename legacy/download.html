Opening an app for the first time?

  1. Double-click the downloaded .zip file, then drag the app into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click the app. When your Mac says it couldn’t check it, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  4. Look for the app’s new button in the top-right of your screen.

Link your Schoology calendar to School Schedule

School Schedule for Mac (version 1.3.7 or newer) can bring in your homework from Schoology. It shows up in the Homework and Plan tabs, and you check things off as you finish them.

  1. On your computer, sign in to Schoology in your web browser.
  2. Click Calendar at the top of the page.
  3. Click Export in the top right of the calendar.
  4. Copy the link it shows. It starts with webcal://
  5. In School Schedule, right-click the clock at the top of your screen and choose Settings, then open the Schoology tab.
  6. Paste the link and click Link Schoology. Your homework shows up in a few seconds.
  7. Click the clock, open Homework, and click the circle next to an assignment when it’s done.

It syncs every 30 minutes, or click Sync now in the Homework tab. Right-click an assignment to change its class or open it in Schoology. Your link works like a password, so don’t share it. It stays on your Mac and is only used to download your calendar from Schoology. Don’t see an Export button? Your school may have turned it off; ask your teacher or school tech help.
