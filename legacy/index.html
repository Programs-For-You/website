How it works

Up and running in three steps

1. Type your email

No password and no account. Just your email, and the downloads open right up.

2. Download and open

Download the app and follow the install guide. The first time, your Mac asks you to allow it.

3. Click the button up top

The app lives in the bar at the top of your screen. Click its button anytime.

Private by design

Your schedule and homework stay on your Mac. Email Assistant and Screen Assistant use the AI built into your Mac, so we never see your email or your screen.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is there a Windows version?

Yes, for School Schedule! Its Windows version is Owen Wang’s original School Schedule Manager. Get it on our Downloads page: check the box to agree to Owen Wang’s Terms of Service, click Windows Download, open the Setup file, and follow the steps. Its source code is on GitHub: github.com/un-undefined/school-schedule-manager. Windows versions of Email Assistant and Screen Assistant are coming soon.

I need help. Who do I contact?

Call (475) 266-1457 or email bradykrupa@programsforyou.com. The install guide also answers the most common questions.

How do I get updates?

Automatically. Our apps update themselves in the background and install new versions when you step away from your computer, so you never have to download them again. You can turn this off in each app’s Settings. More help.

Is it really free?

Yes. The apps are free to download and use. You just type your email.

Why do you want my email?

So we can tell you when there are new apps and updates. That’s it. No password, no spam, and we never sell your information. You can unsubscribe anytime. See our Privacy Policy.

Why does my Mac say it can’t check the app?

Our apps aren’t registered with Apple’s paid developer program yet, so macOS asks you to approve them once. The install guide shows exactly what to click.

What computers do they work on?

School Schedule works on Macs with macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, and on Windows 10 or 11. Email Assistant and Screen Assistant need macOS 26 Tahoe or newer on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). Their Windows versions are coming soon.

Does the Email Assistant send my emails to a server?

No. It connects straight from your Mac to your email and uses the AI built into your Mac. If you choose to add your own Claude API key, the emails being summarized go to Anthropic instead.

Does Screen Assistant watch everything I do?

No. It only looks at your screen when you open it, it never records video, and it doesn’t save pictures. With Apple Intelligence, what it sees stays on your Mac.

Will it ever send an email without asking me?

Never. Every reply waits for you to press Preview & Send.

My school isn’t WHS. Can I still use School Schedule?

Yes. WHS is built in, and you can change the times and classes for any school.

Is this made by Apple?

No. Programs For You is an independent developer and isn’t affiliated with Apple.
