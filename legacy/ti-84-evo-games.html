The games

Grid Pop

You get three shapes at a time. Place them on the 8 by 8 board, and fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clear more than one at once for bonus points. The game ends when none of your shapes fit.

  • ArrowsMove the shape
  • 2ndPlace it
  • enterPick another shape
  • clearQuit

Bounce the ball off your paddle to break every brick. Where the ball hits the paddle sets its angle. Silver bricks take two hits, and each level brings a new pattern and a faster ball.

Instructions

How to put a game on your TI-84 Evo

The games are coming soon. These steps show how it will work when they launch.

What you need

  • A TI-84 Evo graphing calculator.
  • Its USB-C cable.
  • A computer with internet. TI Connect Evo, Texas Instruments’ free tool for sending files to the calculator, runs in your web browser, so there’s nothing to install.
  1. 1

    Download the game

    Go to Downloads, type your email, and click Download next to the game. You’ll get a file like GRIDPOP.py. Don’t rename it: calculator program names can only be 1 to 8 letters.

  2. 2

    Open TI Connect Evo

    Go to TI Connect Evo on Texas Instruments’ website and open it in your browser.

  3. 3

    Connect your calculator

    Plug your TI-84 Evo into the computer with the USB-C cable and turn it on. If your browser asks to let the page connect to your calculator, pick your calculator and click Connect.

  4. 4

    Send the game

    In TI Connect Evo, choose Send files, pick the .py file you downloaded, and send it to your calculator. Do the same for each game you want.

  5. 5

    Play

    On your calculator, press prgm, choose the Python App, then pick the game (like GRIDPOP) from the list and run it. Each game shows its controls on the first screen. Press clear to quit.

Troubleshooting

I don’t see the Python App.

If your calculator’s memory was fully reset, the Python App can disappear. Open TI Connect Evo, connect your calculator, and choose Install OS. Then send the games again.

The game isn’t in the list.

Make sure you sent the .py file and didn’t rename it. Program names have to be 1 to 8 letters, like GRIDPOP, BLOCKBOP, or BLACKJAK. Then try sending it again.

My calculator says it’s out of memory.

Delete programs you don’t use anymore, then send the game again.

The game disappeared from my calculator.

Calculators are often reset before tests, which removes games. Just send them again with TI Connect Evo.

Can I play at school?

Only when your teacher says it’s OK, and never during a test.

Will it work on a TI-84 Plus CE or another calculator?

These games are made for the TI-84 Evo. Other calculators may not be able to run them.
